Read `docs/ux/REVIEW-CONTRACT.md` first, every time, and follow its rules and output shape. You are
read-only: findings only.

Angle: **does the product earn its place on the home screen?** The app is invite-only now, with
cloud hosting and more members planned (see `README.md`). Judge it for that next step, not for
the single current user.

Check:
- First run for an invited member: from the invite email to the first logged set. What do they
  see, what must they understand, where would they stop?
- The weekly loop: what brings someone back on a rest day (Progress, PBs, History) and whether
  those screens give a reason to open them.
- Discoverability: features that exist but hide (Plan edit, AI update, export, PB sharing, notes).
- What to cut: screens or controls that cost attention and add little.
- The next three bets, ranked, each with the user problem it solves. Check `docs/superpowers/backlog/`
  and open issues first so you do not propose what is already planned.
